# Assignment 1.2: Writing an Email to Your Instructor

## Assignment Type

Individual written assignment

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, students should be able to:

* address a university instructor appropriately,
* write a clear and professional academic email,
* use a useful subject line,
* identify themselves and their course section,
* explain the purpose of an email clearly,
* ask a specific question or make a clear request.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **20–30 minutes**

## Task Description

Imagine that you do not understand one requirement of an ICCS 102 exercise and need to ask your instructor for clarification.

Write a short sample email.

Your email must contain:

1. A useful **subject line**.
2. An appropriate **greeting and form of address**.
3. Your name and student ID.
4. Your course and section.
5. A clear explanation of why you are writing.
6. A specific question or request.
7. A short and appropriate closing.

### Addressing the Instructor

Use the title and name that your instructor has asked you to use.

If you are in **Ajarn Tanapuch's section**, appropriate forms of address include:

```text
Dear Ajarn Tanapuch,
```

or

```text
Dear Mr. Wanwarang,
```

If you are in **Ajarn Kritya's section**, appropriate forms of address include:

```text
Dear Ajarn Kritya,
```

or

```text
Dear Professor Kritya,
```

or

```text
Dear Dr. Kritya,
```

Do not assume that you are on a first-name-only basis with an instructor.

For example, starting a university email with only:

```text
Hi Tanapuch
```

or

```text
Hey
```

is not appropriate unless the instructor has specifically told you to address them that way.

### Example Structure

```text
Subject: ICCS102 Section 1 – Question About Week 1 Exercise

Dear Ajarn Tanapuch,

My name is [Your Name], student ID [Your Student ID], from ICCS102 Section 1.

I am writing because I am unsure about [briefly explain what you do not understand].

Could you please clarify whether [your specific question]?

Thank you.

Kind regards,
[Your Name]
[Student ID]
```

You do **not** need to copy this wording. The example only shows the expected structure.

Your own email should be written naturally and should fit the situation you choose.

## Submission and File Naming Format

Write your email in a **Google Doc**.

When finished:

1. Review the document for clarity and correctness.
2. Export or download it as a **PDF**.
3. Submit the PDF to the Google Classroom assignment.

Use this filename:

```text
ICCS102_Week1_Email_[YourFirstName]_[threeLeftMostLettersOfLastName].pdf
```

Example:

```text
ICCS102_Week1_Email_Somchai_Jai.pdf
```
