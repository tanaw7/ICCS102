# Individual Assignment 1.1: Self Introduction

## Assignment Type

Individual in-class and Google Classroom activity

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, students should be able to:

* introduce themselves clearly to the instructor and classmates,
* communicate basic personal and academic background information,
* explain their motivation for joining MUIC and learning programming,
* participate appropriately in a short classroom speaking activity.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **10–15 minutes**

This includes preparation, the short in-class spoken introduction, and the written Google Classroom response.

## Task Description

You will complete this assignment in **two parts**.

### Part 1 – Spoken Self Introduction

Introduce yourself briefly to the class.

Your introduction should cover:

1. Your name.
2. Where you are from and where did you graduate from Highschool.
3. Why you chose to join MUIC.
4. How you spent your time before the term started, during the term break.
5. Why you want to learn programming.

Aim for approximately **1–2 minutes**.

You do not need to prepare a speech or memorize anything. Speak naturally and clearly.

### Part 2 – Written Self Introduction

After the in-class introduction, write your response individually in the **Google Classroom assignment comment**.

Answer the same five points:

1. Your name.
2. Where you are from and where did you graduate from Highschool.
3. Why you chose to join MUIC.
4. How you spent your time during the term break.
5. Why you want to learn programming.

You do not need to write an essay. A short but complete response is sufficient.

## AI Policy

**No AI assistance is allowed for this assignment.**

Do not use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other generative AI tool to write or rewrite your spoken or written self-introduction.

This exercise is about introducing **yourself**, in your own words. You may use the assignment instructions and ask the instructor if any requirement is unclear.

## Submission and File Naming Format

### Submission

Submit the written part directly in the **Google Classroom assignment comment**.

The spoken part will be completed during class.

### File Naming Format

No file upload is required for this assignment.

## Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent | Good | Satisfactory | Unsatisfactory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completion and participation | **3 pts:** Completes both spoken and written parts and addresses all five required points. | **2.5 pts:** Completes both parts with one minor omission. | **2 pts:** Completes the assignment but several required points are brief or missing. | **0–1.5 pts:** One major part is missing, or the response is substantially incomplete. |
| Clarity and relevance | **2 pts:** Introduction is clear, relevant, and gives meaningful information about the student. | **1.5 pts:** Mostly clear and relevant with minor weaknesses. | **1 pt:** Understandable but very limited, vague, or incomplete. | **0–0.5 pts:** Response is unclear, largely irrelevant, or provides very little usable information. |

**Total: 5 points**
