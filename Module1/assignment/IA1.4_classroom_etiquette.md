# Assignment 1.4: Basic Classroom Etiquette and Academic Responsibility

## Assignment Type

Individual written reflection assignment

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, students should be able to:

* explain basic expectations for respectful university classroom behaviour,
* explain why assessment conditions must be consistent,
* explain the importance of deadlines and fairness,
* identify plagiarism and explain why academic integrity matters,
* distinguish individual work from permitted collaboration,
* explain why submission instructions must be followed,
* recognize their responsibility to check course information and ask for help before problems become avoidable emergencies.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **60–90 minutes**. This assignment can be done outside class time.

## Task Description

University students are adults.

That comes with more freedom than school, but it also comes with more responsibility.

This assignment is **not** about memorizing a list of rules. The purpose is to think about **why** these expectations exist and why they matter to you, your classmates, your instructor, the fairness of assessment, and the integrity of the course.

Answer **all eight questions** below.

Each answer must contain **at least 30 words**.

Focus primarily on **why** the behaviour or expectation matters.

Do not simply repeat the rule in different words.

Write in your own words.

### Question 1 – Returning a Greeting

If your instructor enters the classroom and says hello or good morning to the class, or greets you individually, why is it appropriate to acknowledge the greeting and respond?

Explain why this matters even though saying hello has nothing directly to do with programming.

### Question 2 – Headphones and Earphones During Class

Why should headphones or earphones be removed during a lecture, explanation, classroom discussion, or demonstration unless the instructor has specifically allowed their use?

Explain how wearing them can affect your own learning and communication in the classroom.

### Question 3 – Personal Devices During Assessments

During an assessment, why should personal devices such as phones, tablets, smartwatches, earphones, or unauthorized computers be put away unless the instructor has explicitly allowed them?

Consider both **conceptual assessments** and **coding assessments**.

Explain why assessment conditions should be clear and consistent for everyone.

### Question 4 – Deadlines

What does it mean when an assignment has a deadline?

Explain why an instructor cannot simply move or ignore a deadline for one student after the deadline has passed whenever that student asks.

Consider:

* fairness to students who submitted on time,
* course organization,
* grading consistency,
* the integrity of the course.

### Question 5 – Plagiarism

What is plagiarism?

Explain why universities take plagiarism and other forms of academic dishonesty seriously.

Your answer should consider why submitted work must provide trustworthy evidence of what **you** understand or can do.

### Question 6 – Individual Work vs. Collaboration

Sometimes an assignment may allow discussion, teamwork, shared code, or other forms of collaboration while still requiring a particular part to be completed individually.

If a report, explanation, reflection, or answer is explicitly individual, why is it not acceptable to copy another student's writing and submit it as your own?

Would rewriting, paraphrasing, or using a tool to transform another student's individual answer automatically make the work your own? Explain your reasoning.

### Question 7 – Following Submission Instructions

Why does it matter to follow instructions concerning:

* required templates,
* filenames,
* file types,
* folder structure,
* submission location,
* other clearly stated requirements?

Explain why repeatedly ignoring these instructions creates problems even when a student believes the actual content of the work is correct.

### Question 8 – Asking for Help and Checking Course Information

If you are confused about an assignment, when should you ask for clarification?

Explain why it is your responsibility to check:

* Google Classroom,
* assignment instructions,
* announcements,
* instructor feedback,
* other course information,

rather than waiting until after a deadline or assessment to say that you did not know what was required.
