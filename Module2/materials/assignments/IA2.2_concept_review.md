# Individual Assignment 2.2 - Individual Concept Review

## Assignment Type

Individual assignment - **5 points**

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, students should be able to:

* identify the important concepts in Module 2;
* distinguish central concepts from supporting details;
* rank concepts according to importance; and
* prepare evidence for a team discussion.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **20-30 minutes**

## AI Policy

No AI assistance is allowed.

## Task Description

Use the assigned Module 2 materials and review questions provided in the study list.

Create a ranked list of **8-12 important Module 2 concepts**. For each concept:

1. name the concept;
2. explain it briefly in your own words; and
3. explain why it matters when solving a problem.

Then identify:

* the three concepts you consider most important;
* one relationship between two concepts; and
* one question you still have about the module.

You will use this work during the team mind-map activity.

## Submission and File Naming Format

Submit the completed review individually as a PDF.

```text
ICCS102_M2_Review_Individual_[FirstName]_[FirstThreeLettersOfLastName].pdf
```

## Rubric - 5 Points

| Criterion | Complete (1 point) | Mostly complete (0.75 points) | Developing (0.5 points) | Missing or insufficient (0 points) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Concept coverage | Identifies and ranks 8-12 relevant Module 2 concepts. | Identifies 8-12 concepts, but one or two choices are weak, repetitive, or not clearly relevant. | Identifies fewer than 8 meaningful concepts or includes several unsuitable choices. | Provides little or no usable concept list. |
| Explanations | Explains every concept accurately, briefly, and in the student's own words. | Most explanations are accurate and clear, with a small omission or misunderstanding. | Several explanations are vague, incomplete, copied, or inaccurate. | Explanations are mostly missing or show little understanding. |
| Importance and prioritization | Explains why each concept matters and identifies three well-justified priority concepts. | Explains the importance of most concepts and identifies three priorities, but some reasoning is limited. | Gives little explanation of importance or selects priorities without meaningful reasoning. | Does not address importance or identify the three priority concepts. |
| Relationship and remaining question | Describes one meaningful relationship between concepts and provides one relevant question about the module. | Includes both items, but one is vague or underexplained. | Includes only one item or gives responses that show limited thought. | Both items are missing or irrelevant. |
| Correct submission | Submits one readable PDF individually using the required filename and format. | Submits the correct work with one minor filename, identification, or formatting problem. | Submits the work in an incorrect format or with multiple submission problems. | Does not submit the required individual review. |
