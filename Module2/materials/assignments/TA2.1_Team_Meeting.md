# Team Assignment 2.1 - Meet Your Team and Establish Team Expectations

## Assignment Type

Individual preparation followed by a team activity - **5 points**

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, students should be able to:

* introduce themselves to their assigned teammates;
* identify positive and negative teamwork experiences;
* recognize behaviours that support successful collaboration;
* establish clear expectations for the team; and
* agree on an appropriate team name.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **35-45 minutes**

## AI Policy

No AI assistance is allowed.

## Task Description

### Part 1 - Individual Answers

Each team member answers the following before the team consolidates its response:

1. What is your name, programme or major, and year of study?
2. What experience do you have with programming, if any?
3. What do you expect to learn from ICCS102?
4. Describe one good experience you have had working in a team. What made it good?
5. Describe one difficult team experience without naming the people involved. What created the difficulty?
6. What is one action you personally can take to help this team succeed?
7. What can the instructor or facilitator do to make the team-learning experience meaningful?

### Part 2 - Team Discussion

Discuss the individual answers and agree on:

1. three characteristics or actions of a successful team;
2. three risks that could prevent your team from working well;
3. five team expectations that all members agree to follow;
4. one reasonable way the instructor or facilitator can support the team; and
5. a team name.

### Team-Name Rules

The team name:

1. must be in English;
2. must be meaningful;
3. must not be explicit, improper, offensive, discriminatory, or violent;
4. must not contain an inappropriate double meaning;
5. must not simply be a number or a meaningless placeholder such as `Group 5`, `No Name`, or `Anything`; and
6. must receive the instructor's approval.

## Submission and File Naming Format

Each student submits their individual answers in the assignment's **private comment**.

The coordinator submits one team PDF containing:

* the approved team name;
* team members listed alphabetically by first name;
* the consolidated answers from Part 2; and
* the name of the coordinator for this activity.

```text
ICCS102_M2_TeamActivity1_[TeamName].pdf
```
