## Review Questions

The list below can be used to help you study for the first module. All questions in RAT will be drawn from these questions and their answers. However, beware that the actual wording may vary from the list here. In addition, **do adhere to the mandatory resources** when answering questions as your classmates are depending on you to provide good and usable answers for the quiz.

1. List three biggest benefits an undergrad student in any major could gain from learning programming. Briefly justify your selections.
2. Compare and contrast trivial and complex problems.
3. Computational thinking
   A. What is computational thinking?
   B. What are steps of computational thinking? Discuss them briefly here as there are other questions to explore these steps further.
4. Compare and contrast computational thinking and programming.
5. Decomposition
   A. What is decomposition?
   B. What is a typical process of decomposing a problem?
6. Abstraction
   A. What is abstraction in computational thinking?
   B. Is abstraction in programming the same or different to that in computational thinking? Collaborate.
7. Give a real-life, but not necessarily computational, example of each of the topic below
   A. Decomposition
   B. Abstraction
8. Algorithm
   A. What is an algorithm?
   B. What is pseudocode?
   C. What are their relationship to each other and to programming?
9. Why should we learn to program when we can purchase apps for almost anything we wish to do?
10. Which strategies are effective when learning coding (programming)?
11. Programming languages
    A. What is a program?
    B. What is a programming language?
    C. Compare and contrast a programming language to a human language.
12. What is the relationship between computational thinking and programming?
13. Running a program
    A. What does "running" a program do? What are needed for this action? What is the outcome of doing this?
    B. Is running a program the same as executing a program? Collaborate briefly.
14. Compare and contrast learning a programming language and learning a human language.
15. What are syntax and semantics of a programming language? Give an example specific to Python.
